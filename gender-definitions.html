Definitions

Two frameworks the argument leaves open. Both accept everything on the other pages. Pick whichever you find more defensible.

✚

Medical Requirement

Transmedicalist Definition
Many people are frustrated with the belief that anyone can become a man or a woman simply by saying they are. This definition exists to fix that. It anchors the categories in objective, medical reality, ensuring that a change in legal or social sex is routed through a clinical framework and a physical transition, rather than subjective self-declaration.
Woman
A
An adult human assigned female at birth who has neither undergone nor intends to undergo masculinizing medical transition.
OR
B
An adult human assigned male at birth whose persistent internal sense of being female involves gender dysphoria, and who has medically transitioned, or intends to do so, to treat that dysphoria by changing their sex-based characteristics to a female-typical phenotype.
Man
A
An adult human assigned male at birth who has neither undergone nor intends to undergo feminizing medical transition.
OR
B
An adult human assigned female at birth whose persistent internal sense of being male involves gender dysphoria, and who has medically transitioned, or intends to do so, to treat that dysphoria by changing their sex-based characteristics to a male-typical phenotype.
Core Principles
  • Objective Baseline: Sex assigned at birth determines the default category.
  • Medical Exception: Changing categories requires a clinical pathway and physical transition.
  • Measurable Boundary: Categories rely on observable traits, not subjective self-report.

Accurate Scope

  • Default Assignment: Everyone falls into Path A or Path B.
  • No Exclusion: You are either the default (A) or the medical exception (B).
  • Medical Framework: Routed through a clinical pathway. Does not require a formal diagnosis, but distinguishes transition from pure self-ID.
✦

Pure Self-ID

On the other hand, many argue that personal identity is deeply internal and should not be subject to external validation or medical gatekeeping. This framework exists to prioritize individual autonomy. It ensures that a person's internal sense of self is the sole determining factor in their identity, free from financial barriers or societal expectations.
Gender Identity: A person's internal sense of being male, female, some combination of male and female, or neither male nor female. (Merriam-Webster)
Woman
An adult whose persistent internal sense of their own gender identity is female.
Man
An adult whose persistent internal sense of their own gender identity is male.
Non-Binary / Genderfluid
An adult whose persistent internal sense of their own gender identity is a combination of male and female, or neither male nor female.
Core Principles
  • Self-Determination: The individual is the sole authority on their identity.
  • Autonomy: Removes external gatekeeping from medical and legal systems.
  • Inclusivity: Accommodates non-binary identities without medical requirements.
  • Spectrum Inclusion: Acknowledges gender as a non-binary spectrum.
  • Subjective Reality: Internal identity overrides external physical traits.

Accurate Scope

  • Includes Non-Binary: Explicitly includes non-binary, agender, and genderfluid people.
  • Includes: Anyone who sincerely claims this persistent internal sense.
  • Boundary: 100% internal. Relies entirely on self-report. No external verification.
  • Privacy: No public disclosure or medical verification required.
