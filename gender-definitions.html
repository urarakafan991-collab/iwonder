Identifying

Willingly associating with a gender category, whether from birth combined with never having a reason to leave, which is around 99% of people in the category, or associating with it to treat gender dysphoria, or to pursue gender euphoria.

A woman is an adult human person identifying with the female category, regardless of assigned sex at birth.

The vast majority of people in the category were born into the category and never had a reason to leave, while a minority crossed over from another category, some to treat gender dysphoria, others in pursuit of gender euphoria, a felt sense of rightness in the new category. This explanation applies to the other definitions below as well.

A man is an adult human person identifying with the male category, regardless of assigned sex at birth.

A girl is a young human person identifying with the female category, regardless of assigned sex at birth.

A boy is a young human person identifying with the male category, regardless of assigned sex at birth.
