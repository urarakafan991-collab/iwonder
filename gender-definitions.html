Definitions

The terms used across these pages, stated plainly.

Identifying

Willingly associating with a gender category. Either from birth, combined with never having had a reason to leave, which describes around 99% of people in any category. Or associating with it to treat gender dysphoria, or in pursuit of gender euphoria.

A woman is an adult human person identifying with the female category, regardless of assigned sex at birth.

The vast majority of people in the category were born into it and never had a reason to leave. A minority crossed over from another category, some to treat gender dysphoria, others in pursuit of gender euphoria, a felt sense of rightness in the new category. This applies to each of the definitions below as well.

A man is an adult human person identifying with the male category, regardless of assigned sex at birth.

A girl is a young human person identifying with the female category, regardless of assigned sex at birth.

A boy is a young human person identifying with the male category, regardless of assigned sex at birth.
