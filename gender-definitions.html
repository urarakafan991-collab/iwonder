Definitions

The definition the argument produces.

Woman: An adult human who was assigned female at birth and has neither undergone nor intends to undergo masculinizing medical transition; or an adult human who was assigned male at birth, has a formal diagnosis of gender dysphoria, and has medically transitioned or intends to medically transition to a female-typical phenotype.

Man: An adult human who was assigned male at birth and has neither undergone nor intends to undergo feminizing medical transition; or an adult human who was assigned female at birth, has a formal diagnosis of gender dysphoria, and has medically transitioned or intends to medically transition to a male-typical phenotype.

This definition accepts everything argued on the other pages.
