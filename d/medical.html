Medical Requirement

Transmedicalist definition
Categories are anchored in medical reality. Changing category runs through a clinical pathway and a physical transition, not self-declaration.

Woman

A

An adult assigned female at birth who has neither undergone nor intends to undergo masculinizing medical transition.

OR
B

An adult assigned male at birth whose persistent internal sense of being female involves gender dysphoria, and who has medically transitioned, or intends to, toward a female-typical phenotype.

Man

A

An adult assigned male at birth who has neither undergone nor intends to undergo feminizing medical transition.

OR
B

An adult assigned female at birth whose persistent internal sense of being male involves gender dysphoria, and who has medically transitioned, or intends to, toward a male-typical phenotype.
